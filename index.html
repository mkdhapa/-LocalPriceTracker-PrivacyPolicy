Privacy Policy for LocalPriceTracker

Effective Date: March 2026

LocalPriceTracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect data necessary to provide and improve local price tracking features:

  • Anonymous User Identifier (Firebase Auth): When you open LocalPriceTracker, an anonymous, unique user ID (UID) is generated automatically via Firebase Anonymous Authentication. No email address, password, phone number, or real identity is collected.
  • User-Generated Price Submissions: When you record or submit a local produce price, we store the commodity name, market name, city, price per unit, shop name (optional), and timestamp in Cloud Firestore associated with your anonymous user ID.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Display crowdsourced local commodity prices and price trends to app users.
  • Track your contribution count locally in the app.
  • Enforce document ownership rules (allowing users to edit or delete their own submitted prices).
  • Fetch government commodity reference prices via AGMARKNET API (data.gov.in).

3. Third-Party Services

LocalPriceTracker integrates third-party services that operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Used for secure user session management and cloud data synchronization. Google Privacy Policy
  • AGMARKNET / Data.gov.in API: Used to fetch official government commodity prices. Data.gov.in Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage & Security

Your price submissions are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore. We implement security rules so users can only modify or delete their own price contributions. Because authentication is anonymous, deleting the app or clearing app data will disconnect your access to past contributions.

5. Children's Privacy

LocalPriceTracker does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Data Deletion & Rights

You can delete any price entry you submitted directly within the app by long-pressing or tapping the delete button on the price card. You can also request complete data removal by contacting us.
